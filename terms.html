1. Orders & payment

  • All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦) and include the food cost; delivery fees are calculated by distance and shown before you pay.

2. Delivery

  • Delivery fee is a flat ₦1,100 per kilometre of the mapped route from our kitchen to your address, and is shown before checkout.
  • Delivery times are estimates and may vary with traffic, weather and order volume.
  • Please provide an accurate address and reachable phone number; repeated failed delivery attempts may incur additional charges.

3. Operating hours

We accept orders daily from approximately 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM (West African Time). Orders placed outside these hours cannot be prepared until the kitchen reopens. Staff may also manually close ordering in exceptional circumstances.

4. Food & allergies

Meals are prepared fresh. If you have a food allergy or specific dietary requirement, please state it clearly in the order note or tell us by phone before ordering.

5. Refunds & cancellations

If an order cannot be fulfilled due to our error or a failed payment verification, we will refund the verified payment. Once preparation has begun, cancellations may be limited; please contact us promptly by phone or WhatsApp.

Do not use the site to place fraudulent orders, submit false information, or attempt to disrupt the service. Such orders will be cancelled and may be reported.
