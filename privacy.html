Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Cyril's Foods (“we”, “us”) operates this ordering website for our restaurant based at 26 College Rd, Ifako-Ijaiye, Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you place an order and how we use it. By using this site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, delivery address (and the map coordinates we look up), the items you order, and any optional delivery note.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Paystack. We do not see or store your card numbers or bank details; we receive only a transaction reference and payment status.
  • Technical data: basic request information (such as IP address and browser type) needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To prepare, verify and deliver your order, including distance-based delivery pricing.
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about your order if needed.
  • To process and confirm payments and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the kitchen ordering system secure and working.

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with service providers necessary to fulfil your order — primarily Paystack (payments) and our delivery/communications channels.

3. Legal basis & data retention

We process order information because it is necessary to fulfil your order and because of our legitimate interest in running a secure food business under Nigerian law. Order records are retained only as long as needed for delivery, accounting and dispute resolution, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

4. Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and delivery address for convenience, and (for staff) a kitchen sign-in token. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Clearing your browser data removes this information.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you by contacting us. You can also ask us not to use your details for any purpose beyond fulfilling your order.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email cyrilsfood@gmail.com or call 0808 198 8184.
